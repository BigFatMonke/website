Our Mission

Closing the gap between performance and skin health.

TheUVProject is a youth-led nonprofit dedicated to protecting student athletes from preventable UV exposure by promoting lifelong sun-safe habits. Through student-led chapters, practical education, and partnerships with athletic programs, we make sun protection accessible, practical, and second nature for young athletes — on the field, the court, and the course.
